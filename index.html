CTA Trust Board

A Chicago transit app that tells riders whether to believe the arrival on screen, and shows rerouted buses that CTA's own tracker hides.

Every screen and state the app has, drawn as design mockups at iPhone size with illustrative data.

Home and planning

Home answers the morning question first: when to leave. Stop search runs on the phone against every stop and station, so results appear as you type.

Swipe sideways for more.

Stops and stations

Every arrival carries one line that says why to believe it. Trains are the Chicago flag's red stars: solid when tracked live, dashed and hollow when they exist only on the schedule.

Swipe sideways for more.

Lines

The whole L line as one stripe with every train on it. The motion only shows state, and it turns off for riders who prefer reduced motion.

Swipe sideways for more.

Trips

One-seat rides from walkable stops. The planner never boards a rider at a stop the route is skipping, and each option says where its time comes from.

Swipe sideways for more.

Following a trip

After Start, the app follows the ride through each phase and never shows more certainty than it has. The screen stays on while a trip is open.

Swipe sideways for more.

Commutes

A commute is a saved trip with an arrive-by time and days. Each time, the planner picks the ride, and Home shows when to leave. Everything is saved on the device only.

Offline, loading, and errors

CTA's feeds can be slow or down, so every screen has a plan for missing data. Old times are labelled with when they were fetched, never shown as current.

Swipe sideways for more.

Trust labels

Every arrival carries one line that says why to believe it. The label comes from what ran in that hour slot over the last 10 days, so the copy always describes the slot, never one train.

Design system

The visual direction is called Flag, after the Chicago city flag: bright, civic, and readable in sunlight on an outdoor platform.

Color

  • Flag blue#B3DDF2Bands, track, the lake
  • Star red#E4002BVehicles
  • Ink#0E2A3BText, detours, "You"
  • Ink muted#3D5A6CSecondary text
  • Alert#B0001FShaky, unlikely, not stopping
  • Alert background#FDECEEReroute banner
  • Map ground#F4F8FALand on the map
  • Rule#EDF3F6Row dividers

Type

Schibsted Grotesk, self-hosted, with tabular numerals on minute counts so they never jump sideways as they tick down.

Vehicles
